01What Nine Mile does

It quotes the prices that let ETFs trade.

Market making

When an investor buys or sells an ETF, someone has to be on the other side. A market maker shows a buy price and a sell price all day, so the trade can happen close to what the fund is worth. Nine Mile does this with its own technology, from Sydney and Shanghai.

  • ETF market making The lead service

    Issuers appoint Nine Mile to quote their fund from listing day. Their investors get a fair price, and the issuer gets a partner to call.

  • Direct liquidity

    Asset managers and brokers call the desk to trade a large ETF order at one price, without moving the price on screen.

  • Its own technology

    The trading platform is built in house. The current site says "nothing is legacy".

  • A place to build a career

    Traders, engineers and operators. The brand idea is "Future Makers", and most of the brand guideline speaks to recruits.

03The problem with the site and v1.2

It introduces a company. It doesn't start a conversation.

  • Today's site never says "ETF"

    It describes a market maker in general terms. An issuer can't tell the site is for them. The newest insight is from February 2024.

  • v1.2 reads like a service template

    Hero, numbers, services, benefits, steps, logos. Each section is fine, and nothing connects one to the next.

  • The globe carries no data

    Pins and arcs decorate the hero. The brand already has a better device: a sphere made of live figures.

  • Students get one card

    A third of the audience meets a single teaser near the footer, with no trader or developer in sight.

  • One door for every lead

    "Talk to our team" goes to a form. No named person, no desk number up front, and nothing for a visitor who isn't ready to call.

Keep from v1.2
The sitemap, the footer, the CTA labels and the issuer-first copy
Quality bar
The Endear proposal by Konpo: for its storytelling and wireframe craft, not its look

Tell one trading day, and give each audience its moment.

The core message (draft), shared by A, B and C

  • A story with a spine

    Hang the page on something real: the clock, the visitor's role or the record. Each section picks up where the last one ended.

  • A door for each audience

    Business Development for issuers, the desk line for brokers, open roles for students. Always the same three labels.

  • People you can reach

    A named BD lead with a photo and the desk's direct number, in the page and not only in the footer.

  • A globe that works

    The brand's Data Device as a sphere of real figures: exchanges, local time and ETFs quoting now.

  • A way to stay in touch

    A desk note by email, for the visitor who is interested and not yet ready to talk.

FAQ

Before You Get in Touch

What does a market maker do for my ETF?

We post buy and sell prices on your ETF throughout the trading day. Investors can always trade, and the price stays close to the value of the fund's holdings.

How do we appoint Nine Mile?

An intro call, due diligence, a market making agreement, then live quotes. For a new fund, we start the conversation well before listing day.

How large an order can the desk price?

Call the desk with the fund, side and size. We price blocks in the ETFs we cover during ASX trading hours.

Which markets do you cover?

ASX and Cboe Australia, plus ETF trading in Hong Kong and Taiwan. Europe follows when Amsterdam opens in 2027.

Are you hiring?

Yes. We have open roles in trading, technology and operations, for graduates and experienced hires.
